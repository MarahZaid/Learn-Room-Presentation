What we built

A working prototype, built and tested in the lab.

Smart access & attendance

An ID scan or PIN opens the door only for enrolled students during their lecture. A camera recognises faces to record attendance.

Smart lighting & climate

Lights respond to presence and daylight (Auto, Manual and Projector modes). Fan speed follows temperature; high CO2 triggers alerts.

Smart adaptive chairs

Motorised chairs rearrange themselves on command.

  • Lecture
  • Exam
  • Group
“Norma…”

Norma, the voice assistant

Wakes when it hears its name and announces CO2 alarms, door events and fan status.
